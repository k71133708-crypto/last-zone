Privacy Policy - Last Zone

Last Updated: 25november2026

English

Last Zone does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

The game saves your progress (coins, weapons, characters, best score, and settings) only on your own device, using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data will reset your progress.

The game does not contain advertisements or third-party tracking. It does not use analytics.

Children: The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
